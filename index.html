About NetAtlas

NetAtlas grew out of OSI Flow, an interactive OSI model explorer. It keeps that explorer at its core and adds topic decks covering ports, addressing, cabling, routing, switching, wireless, services, security, troubleshooting, QoS, WAN/VPN, and automation. Progress is saved locally in your browser. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to a server.

The OSI (Open Systems Interconnect) model is a conceptual framework that standardizes the functions of a communication system into seven abstraction layers. Each layer provides services to the layer above and consumes services from the layer below.

As data travels downward (encapsulation), each layer adds its own headers and trailers. As data travels upward (decapsulation) at the receiving end, those headers are stripped away, revealing the original payload.
